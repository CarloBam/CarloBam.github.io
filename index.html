About

I work in people analytics at Woolworths South Africa, where I've spent four years turning HR data into decisions: reporting, risk tools and predictive models for workforce planning.

My path into data didn't start in a maths department. I came through human resource management, which taught me something many technical teams learn late: a model only matters if the people affected by it can understand it and act on it. That's why I care about interpretability, fairness and clear communication as much as accuracy.

I'm now moving deeper into data science and machine learning engineering, with a particular interest in financial services. This site is where I share what I'm building and learning along the way.

Who Works in Data? QLFS Occupations Study

Predicting participation in computing and quantitative occupations in South Africa from Quarterly Labour Force Survey microdata, 2013 to 2024. Compares penalised logistic regression, LightGBM and an Explainable Boosting Machine on about 1.5 million person-year records. An early finding: the occupational classification itself is unstable over time.
