About

I work in people analytics at Woolworths South Africa, where I've spent four years turning HR data into decisions: data models, reporting, risk tools and predictive models for workforce planning.

My path into data didn't start in a computer science department. I came through human resource management, which taught me something many technical teams learn late: data only matters if the people relying on it can trust it and act on it. That's why I care about data quality, clear lineage and honest communication as much as clever code.

I'm now moving into data engineering, building the pipelines, warehouses and transformations that analytics and machine learning depend on, with a particular interest in financial services. This site is where I share what I'm building and learning along the way.

Who Works in Data? QLFS Occupations Study

Twelve years of Quarterly Labour Force Survey microdata, deduplicated into about 1.5 million person-year records, then used to predict participation in computing and quantitative occupations. An early finding: the occupational classification itself is unstable over time, a data quality problem before it is a modelling one.
